## Description
BuildVerse AI is an AI-powered project planning assistant that transforms ideas into structured, beginner-friendly development roadmaps. Users can describe their project, select their coding experience, and choose a preferred technology or request recommendations.

The application uses the open-weight Qwen 2.5 3B model through Ollama to generate project summaries, key features, development phases, actionable tasks, estimated effort, suggested tools, and potential risks with mitigation strategies. An interactive progress tracker helps users monitor completed tasks.

BuildVerse AI aims to bridge the gap between having an idea and knowing how to implement it, empowering students and beginner developers to plan software projects with greater clarity and confidence.

## Problem Statement
Students and beginner developers often struggle to turn their ideas into practical software projects. Choosing suitable technologies, identifying essential features, organizing development tasks, estimating effort, and anticipating risks can be overwhelming. BuildVerse AI addresses this challenge by providing personalized, structured roadmaps that make project planning easier and more accessible.

## Solution
BuildVerse AI converts project descriptions into step-by-step development plans tailored to the user's experience and technology preferences. It combines open-weight AI with a simple web interface to help users understand what to build, which tools to consider, and how to organize their work.

## Reliability Considerations
Generated roadmaps are AI recommendations and may require human review. Reliability can be evaluated using different project prompts and checking the relevance of features, technology suitability, task feasibility, and clarity of assumptions.

## Limitations
- Generated recommendations may be incomplete or inaccurate.
- Time estimates are approximate.
- The application currently runs locally and requires the appropriate Python environment, Ollama installation, and Qwen 2.5 3B model.
- The application should not be considered a replacement for developer judgment or project-specific technical review.
